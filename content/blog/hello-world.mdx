# Welcome to my blog!

This is my first post using **Velite** as a content management system for my portfolio website.

## Why I Built This

I wanted a place to share my thoughts, document my projects, and showcase the products I've been working on. This blog will be the central hub for all of that.

## What to Expect

Here's what you can look forward to:

- **Technical deep-dives**: Breaking down complex problems and solutions
- **Project showcases**: Behind-the-scenes looks at my work
- **Product updates**: News about tools and apps I'm building
- **Learning journeys**: Sharing what I learn along the way

## The Tech Stack

This portfolio is built with:

- **Next.js 16** - The React framework for production
- **Velite** - Type-safe content management
- **Tailwind CSS** - Utility-first styling
- **TypeScript** - Because types matter
